    Method Overview

    1Problem

    Existing zero-shot VLM approaches (GVL, TOPReward) suffer from confirmation bias and hallucinated progress in failed runs when estimating rewards.
    Post-trained alternatives (Robometer, RoboReward, SOLE-R1) require expensive real-world data.

    2Method

    To address this, D&A decouples progress estimation into a Describe and Assess stage, first describing what happened in the video before assessing progress with respect to a success criterion.

    D&A Rich Feedback Improves Code-as-Policies

    Allowing Code-as-Policies (CaP) to iterate over its solutions improves performance from 11.4% (no history) to 40.6% (history). Adding D&A's rich language feedback achieves 51.4%, showcasing how rich semantic feedback provides a more effective signal than scalar rewards for downstream refinement.
